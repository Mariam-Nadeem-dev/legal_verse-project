Legal Verse — Website Plan

1. Who Is This Website For?

Legal Verse is a website being developed for my father, who is a lawyer in Pakistan. It is intended to introduce his legal work and provide visitors with useful legal information.

2. What Should Visitors Be Able to Do?

Visitors should be able to understand the purpose of the website, learn about the lawyer and his verified legal services, explore useful legal resources, and find a way to contact him.

3. Research and Facts

- The website is intended for a real lawyer in Pakistan. Source: Information provided by the website owner.
- The Constitution of Pakistan, 1973, is an important legal document in Pakistan. Source: Official Pakistani government legal resources.
- The Supreme Court of Pakistan is the country's highest court. Source: Official Supreme Court of Pakistan website.
- The National Assembly of Pakistan is part of Pakistan's parliamentary system. Source: Official National Assembly of Pakistan website.

Before publication, I will verify legal information against reliable official sources and include the relevant source links on the website where appropriate. I will not publish unverified qualifications, services, contact details, or legal claims.

4. Website Section Order

The website will follow a mobile-first layout:

1. Header and navigation
2. Hero section introducing Legal Verse
3. About the lawyer
4. Legal services, after confirming the details
5. Legal information and resources
6. Contact section
7. Footer

5. Look and Feel

- Three words: Professional, trustworthy, clear.
- Colors: Deep green, white, and gold accents.
- Fonts: Clear, readable fonts.
- Layout: Simple sections, readable text, suitable images, and clear navigation.
- Reason: The design should feel professional and remain easy to use on phones, tablets, and computers.

6. How I Will Use AI

I used AI to help me understand and write HTML and CSS, organize the website sections, and improve the responsive layout. I will review the generated code, test the website in a browser, and check that the information is accurate.

One issue I noticed was that changing the hero background gradient made the background look more washed out. I noticed the visual difference and decided not to continue changing that part until the rest of the website was ready.

7. Unknowns and Risks

- Confirm the lawyer's preferred name, qualifications, services, and contact details before publishing them.
- Verify legal information using reliable sources.
- Check that images load and have suitable alternative text.
- Test the website at mobile and desktop widths, from 320px to 1440px.
- Check that there is no horizontal scrolling on small screens.
- Protect private information and obtain permission before publishing personal details.
- Check that the GitHub Pages website is accessible through its live link.
